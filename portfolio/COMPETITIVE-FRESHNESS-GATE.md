# Competitive Freshness Gate — Binding commercial admission rule

**Effective decision:** 2026-10-10. **Owner:** portfolio owner. **Scope:** all candidate products, offers, marketplaces and material commercial feature expansions. This is a portfolio-level strategic gate, **not a Dot execution authorization**.

## Four compulsory questions before commercial promotion

1. **Current alternatives:** which strongest native, paid, open-source and free alternatives solve the buyer's job *today*? Provide primary dated sources and access/pricing restrictions. Never describe a competitor as missing a feature without evidence.
2. **Commodity overlap:** is the proposal wholly or largely an existing feature, template, or workflow available elsewhere? Distinguish superficial overlap from exact functional equivalence.
3. **Differentiation for a buyer:** what **specific economic outcome**, for a **named buying role/use case**, is objectively better? Demonstrate with reproducible comparative evidence, including integration and support friction—not architectural complexity.
4. **Recent demand:** what independent buyer signal supports *willingness to pay* for that difference, and through which credible zero-upfront-cost distribution path could the buyer discover it? Forum posts and job listings show pain but are not sales or proof of payment.

## Admission record

Every material commercial proposal must reference a dated review with: product and head/version; buyer and use case; existing spending/alternative; comparative feature matrix; evidence sources and source dates; pricing/rights/distribution constraints; cost to adopt; measured buyer signal; unknowns; falsification conditions; reviewer; next recheck date or condition.

Use one of these decision outcomes:
- `PASS_TO_BOUNDED_TEST`: dated competitor review, defensible buyer outcome, plausible accessible distribution **and a concrete independent purchasing signal**; authorizes **requesting** a separately admitted experiment, not executing it.
- `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`: potential difference exists, but buyer need, spend, delivery or distribution is not validated; commercial expansion cannot proceed.
- `COMMERCIAL_HOLD`: commodity overlap or costly adoption threatens the position and no verified buyer evidence compensates; stop allocating selling/build effort until a specific re-entry test passes.
- `COMMERCIAL_CLOSE`: demonstrably obsolete/noncompetitive or uneconomic after fair real-world evidence; archive commercial direction with rationale. Do not infer this from lack of account access.
- `UNKNOWN`: comparison itself has insufficient coverage; never pretend this is an approval.

## Enforcement

Before authorizing new feature building *for commercial positioning*, product launch, paid campaign, marketplace packaging or a "default first product" selection, require `PASS_TO_BOUNDED_TEST` and a separate valid NOW/READY/authority admission under the Dot operating contract. A strategic PASS is **necessary but not sufficient** for execution. This does not block maintenance, contractual support, urgent safety fixes, historical evidence retention, self-use research or independently approved technical priorities.

**Independent review:** the initiating model's enthusiasm, a green CI run, a price on a listing, GitHub Stars, a competitor's marketing page, and the user's agreement are *not* buyer proof. Correct user assumptions when evidence conflicts. Preserve counter-evidence and limitations.

**Fast feedback:** lightweight desk review first; invest in comparative testing only for a promising buyer-supported case. Recheck market material changes before spending or publishing. No arbitrary waiting periods or fabricated demand thresholds.

**Minimal-capital rule:** do not presume Stripe, overseas incorporation, paid advertisements, paid services, or hardware availability. Evaluate the owner's existing permissible channels and payout arrangements without exposing account data.

**Authority:** Human retains merges, publication, financial commitments and external outreach. Dots remains OBSERVE-only under the existing separate mandate.
