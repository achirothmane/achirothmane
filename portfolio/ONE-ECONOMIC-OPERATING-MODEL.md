## One objective, not two competing businesses

> Build reusable assets we own; use them to produce evidence-backed outcomes; identify a buyer with existing spend; package and distribute a repeatable offer; verify a paid transaction and delivery; improve unit economics; automate only the proven repeated work.

**Primary business:** owned, compounding digital products, data products with cleared rights, productized tools, and (once validated) subscriptions. **Not the default:** becoming a full-time bespoke contractor. A bounded paid service can be an *experiment* or temporary cash bridge if it produces reusable evidence/capability, has a defined scope, and is explicitly approved. It must not create an untracked second strategic lane.

Technical capabilities remain deliberately broad and self-use-first. **One commercial experiment at a time** is a work-in-progress limit, not permanent narrowing of the technical portfolio or its future markets.

## Single feedback loop

1. **Own/build:** use an existing capable repository; avoid opening a new repository unless clearly justified.
2. **Prove outcome:** reproduce actual capability and failure boundary; do not mistake green CI for customer utility.
3. **Locate a buyer:** identify an actual economic pain, current alternative, potential budget, decision-maker and reachable distribution path.
4. **Package:** explain a bounded deliverable, setup effort, buyer responsibility, price, support and refund terms; check licenses/data rights.
5. **Distribute:** test free/owned channels first; avoid reliance on unsolicited mass outreach or upfront ad spend.
6. **Collect evidence:** record genuine checkout completion and independently verifiable delivery before counting revenue; a listing, price, click, or promise is not a sale.
7. **Repeat:** seek evidence of a second independent buyer for the same product without one-off rebuild.
8. **Automate:** only automate stable, repeatable operations within a bounded, reversible authority envelope.

## First commercial experiment — select by independent freshness, not familiarity

**Mandatory gate:** [Competitive Freshness Gate](COMPETITIVE-FRESHNESS-GATE.md). A credible buyer outcome, current alternatives, practical distribution path and independent purchasing signal must be documented *before* a commercial candidate is promoted to a separately admitted experiment. A green CI suite or published listing does not pass.

**2026-10-10 correction:** The former default `releaseguard-n8n` $39 Production Pack candidate is **withdrawn** after a [source-backed market assessment](reviews/releaseguard-competitive-freshness-2026-10-10.md): **`COMMERCIAL_HOLD`**. It retains a technically differentiated live read-only canary, but generic release tooling is competitive and no independent buyer has validated willingness to pay for its incremental value. Do not describe this as total feature parity, absence of sales, or an irreversible product death.

**Current first paid-offer selection: NOT_SELECTED.** Do not silently replace it with n8n freelance work, a data product, CI tools, a new SaaS or another repo without the same gate. Commercial candidate screening is read-only; it grants no portfolio NOW/READY item, Dot tool authority or spend.

**After a product passes the gate:** verify packaging/checkout/payout/delivery, compare competitors again before publication, then ask separately to admit one zero-upfront-cost distribution experiment with attributable measurements. Start a 14-day observation window **only after qualified exposure**. Do not interpret a period without distribution as proof of no demand.

**Learning gates:**
- `UNKNOWN`: no verified exposure/checkout/payment evidence; do not infer zero demand or success.
- `INTEREST`: qualified buyer behavior exists, but payment is not verified; improve positioning, friction and offer rather than build a platform.
- `FIRST_PAID`: independently confirmed external payment **and** satisfactory delivery; record cash fees, refunds, support time and provenance.
- `REPEATABLE_SIGNAL`: at least two distinct real buyers purchase substantially the same deliverable without bespoke rebuilding; an early signal, not sustainable profitability.
- `PIVOT_OR_STOP`: enough relevant exposure and feedback with no buyer, or unacceptable support economics; compare alternatives before more engineering.

Absolute traffic and conversion targets must be tied to actual channel evidence, not invented guarantees.

## Minimal unit-economics record

Record per transaction/test window, with dated evidence: distribution channel; qualified exposures; product-page visits; inquiries; checkout starts; paid transactions; verified gross receipts; payment/platform fees; refunds; provider/hosting cost; delivery minutes; support minutes; net cash contribution; follow-on purchases; reusable assets gained; rights/license restrictions. Treat missing as `UNKNOWN`, never as zero.

**Cash contribution** = collected revenue − refunds − payment/platform fees − attributable cash operating costs. **Economic profit** additionally accounts for valued human effort and other relevant costs; do not call cash contribution full net profit. Keep identifiable customer data, account access and payment evidence in a private, authorized store, not in this public portfolio.

## Explicit role of each component

- **Data Engine:** produce validated, provenance-preserving datasets/reports where rights allow; do not assert unrestricted resale rights.
- **AI Native Engineering:** future owned editorial/distribution surface; not to be described as deployed until proven.
- **Marketing OS:** candidate distribution/analytics operations; scoped engineering CI does not prove operational sending, compliance, or production readiness.
- **Agent Deal Exchange:** optional future commerce component, *not* a required dependency and not proven settlement.
- **Agent Runtime / single Dots:** portfolio observation and proposals first; no claim of profitable autonomous management.
- **Existing GitHub products:** test concrete offers from their own evidence and buyer requirements; no forced platform merger.

## Anti-duality decision rules

- **Services vs products:** service work is a time-boxed validation/cash bridge only; primary compounding path remains repeatable owned products.
- **Technical breadth vs focus:** maintain broad capabilities, but admit only one paid-offer experiment concurrently.
- **Fast tests vs long validation:** run rapid bounded tests of value and distribution; require longitudinal evidence before claims of operational autonomy or sustainable margins.
- **Manager vs executor:** Dots coordinates under evidence/authority gates; product engines execute; humans retain external and economic finality.
- **Internal architecture vs buyer value:** do not sell a project graph, a fork, or agent autonomy as value; sell a measured buyer outcome.
- **Commercial intent vs proof:** potential prices, listings, traffic and design sketches cannot be upgraded to customer payment or revenue.

## Safeguards

- No required upfront paid tools, advertising, domains, model APIs, or cloud infrastructure without explicit owner approval.
- No invented user testimonials, buyer commitments, revenues, deployments, conversion rates or certification.
- No automated spam; follow privacy, opt-in and marketplace/platform rules.
- No publish/merge of private repository facts, customer data, account/payment identifiers or proprietary evidence into this public portfolio.
- Every proposed new paid feature must pass competitor freshness, observed buyer pain, distribution, packaging, commercial depth, asset compounding and affordability gates.
